## What is new

- **Mock interview**: a clock runs while you answer out loud or type. Then a four-part structure guide, then the model answer beside your own.
- **Live-coding rehearsal**: pick up to five coding tasks, one clock for the set (30, 45 or 60 min), prompts to think out loud, and a summary per task.
- **Pick topics by role or job ad**: presets for Manual QA, Automation, SDET and Lead, or paste a job ad and the page picks the topics it names. The ad stays in your browser.
- **My cards**: add your own questions, for example from a company's interview, with one note per company. They go into Export progress.
- **Works offline on the website**: after one visit, https://bartech-lab.github.io/qa-interview-bank/ opens without a network.
- **Locator drill** moved into Learn (Playwright topic). Its practice pages now show only the exercise.
- **questions.json**: every question with its id, topic and tier, attached to this release for reuse (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Content checks run on every change, and the Source links are checked weekly. See CONTRIBUTING.md to propose a question.

Saved progress from earlier versions loads unchanged.
