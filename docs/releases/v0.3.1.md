## What is fixed

- **Company notes follow your cards**: when a company's last card moves to another company, its note moves with it, or is added below that company's note. Notes that v0.3.0 left behind with no card are removed when the page loads or imports progress.
- **Search in Learn** looks only at the text you see, plus a card's company. Words such as "core" or "topic" no longer list every question.
- **Find topics** (job ad) is disabled while it matches, so a second ad typed meanwhile is never replaced by the first ad's result.
- **Install as an app**: PNG icons for phones and for Safari before version 26.
- **Phones**: the matched job-ad words sit under each topic name, and the rehearsal's think-aloud prompt gets the full width.

Saved progress from earlier versions loads unchanged, apart from those company notes with no card.
